1. What Waypoint is

Waypoint helps you keep track of life on a GLP-1 medication. You can:

  • log doses and get reminders
  • see a schedule and an estimated medication level
  • log food with optional AI estimates
  • track protein, water, weight, activity and mood
  • follow your progress toward goals you set

Waypoint has no accounts. Your data is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud, as described in the Privacy Policy.

3. AI-assisted estimates

  • AI output is not nutritional, dietary or medical advice.

  • AI features may change, be limited (for example, a daily number of AI estimates per device) or become unavailable at times. You can always enter values yourself.
