Waypoint Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Waypoint iOS app (“Waypoint”, “the app”) handles, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean the developer that publishes Waypoint, based in Nigeria and named on its App Store page.

Summary of key points

Do we have your data? Almost never. Waypoint has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you log is stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud, which we can’t access.

Do we handle sensitive information? Yes, on your device. Your medication, doses, weight, food and mood are health information. The app processes them only to give you its features. They are never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI.

Does anything leave your device? Only when you ask the AI to estimate a meal, a nutrition label, an ingredient or an activity. Then the text or photo you gave is sent to an AI provider to get the estimate. Nothing else about you goes with it.

Do we track you? No. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising identifier.

How can you control it? Edit or delete any entry, delete everything with Settings → Delete All Data, turn iCloud, Apple Health and notifications on or off, or skip AI and enter values yourself. You can also email us at any time.

Medical disclaimer

Waypoint is a self-tracking companion. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnose conditions, or recommend, prescribe or change doses. Calorie, macro and activity estimates, daily targets and the medication level chart are approximations and may be inaccurate. Always follow the guidance of your prescriber or healthcare professional.

1. Who we are

Waypoint is an independent app for people taking GLP-1 medications. It is published by a developer based in Nigeria and named on its App Store page, who is the data controller for the limited personal data we handle ourselves. In practice that means the emails you send us and AI requests while they pass through to our providers.

Waypoint is built to keep data with you. The app follows a few principles: collect only what a feature needs, keep it on your device or in your own iCloud, process it only for the purpose you used it for, and let you delete it at any time.

4. AI food and activity estimates

When you describe a meal, scan a meal or nutrition label, add or rename an ingredient, or describe a workout, Waypoint sends that text or photo to an AI service so it can estimate calories, macros or effort.

Requests go through our AI provider’s gateway (Rork, routed through the Vercel AI Gateway) to a language model from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. Only the description or photo and the instructions for the estimate are sent. Your name, health profile, medication, weight and other logs are not.

These providers process the request to return an answer and handle it under their own privacy terms. Some may keep requests for a limited time, for example to prevent abuse. We don’t keep a copy.

AI is optional. You can enter calories and macros yourself on the Enter macros tab, and edit any estimate before saving it.

5. Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Waypoint reads your step count and active energy to show the calories you burn. It only reads and never writes to Apple Health.

Information from Apple Health stays on your device. It is never sent to the AI service, never shared with anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

You can stop access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Waypoint.

6. Camera, photos and notifications

Camera and photos. These are optional and used only when you scan food or a nutrition label. Photos you take or pick are used for that estimate and, if you log the food, kept on your device with it. Waypoint doesn’t browse or upload your photo library.

Notifications. Dose, protein, weight and calorie reminders are scheduled on your device. No reminder content is sent anywhere. You can change them in Settings → Reminders or turn them off in iOS Settings.

7. Where data is stored and for how long

  • On your device: until you delete the entry, use Delete All Data, or delete the app.
  • In your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit): if you’re signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Waypoint, your data syncs there so it’s backed up and appears on your other devices. Apple stores it under your iCloud account and Apple’s privacy terms, and we can’t access it. It stays until you delete it. Deleting the app on one device doesn’t remove it from iCloud.
  • Device backups: if your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, Waypoint’s data is included under Apple’s terms, as with any app.
  • AI requests: not stored by us. Providers keep them only as their own terms allow.
  • Support emails: kept for as long as needed to help you and handle any follow-up, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

9. International transfers

Apple and our AI providers may process data in the United States and other countries outside where you live. Where data protection law requires it, these transfers rely on safeguards the providers offer, such as adequacy decisions (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified) and Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Security

Waypoint keeps your data in the app’s private storage on iOS, protected by your device passcode and iOS data protection. iCloud data is encrypted by Apple in transit and on its servers. AI requests are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because we don’t run a server that stores your data, there is no central Waypoint database of user information to breach.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keeping your device passcode and Apple ID secure helps protect your data.

11. Your choices and rights

Most of your data is in your hands, so you can act on it directly:

  • See and correct: everything you log is visible in the app and can be edited.
  • Delete: delete any single entry, or use Settings → Delete All Data to permanently remove everything Waypoint stored, on this device and in your iCloud (so on your other devices too). This also cancels its reminders. It doesn’t cancel a subscription; do that in your App Store settings.
  • Remove iCloud copies after deleting the app: iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Waypoint.
  • Keep data on one device: turn off iCloud for Waypoint in iOS Settings.
  • Withdraw permissions: turn off Apple Health, camera or notification access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Avoid AI: enter values yourself instead of using the AI estimate.

For anything else, including questions about support emails we hold, email support.waypoint.app@gmail.com. Requests are free. We may ask for information to confirm the request is yours, and we reply within one month. Because data you log isn’t on any server of ours, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side beyond support emails.

12. People in Nigeria, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy
  • have it corrected or erased
  • restrict or object to its processing
  • data portability
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you. Waypoint makes no such decisions.

The legal bases we rely on are set out in section 3. You can contact us to exercise your rights. We respond within one month, which may be extended by two months for complex requests, and we’ll tell you if it is.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority: in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC); in the EU, the authority in your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO); in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). We’d appreciate the chance to help first.

13. California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, you have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose
  • delete it
  • correct it
  • opt out of its sale or sharing
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights

In the last 12 months, the only personal information we may have received is the content of AI requests (passed to the providers in section 8 to generate an estimate) and support emails (identifiers and the content of your message). Health information you log stays on your devices and in your iCloud.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we use sensitive personal information only to provide the app’s features. To make a request, email us. An authorised agent may act for you with written permission.

14. Children

Waypoint is meant for adults taking GLP-1 medications and is not directed to anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

15. Cookies, tracking and advertising

The app has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and doesn’t use cookies. It doesn’t ask for permission to track you, because it doesn’t. This page doesn’t set cookies either.

Links to other websites, such as Apple’s terms or a health resource, are governed by those sites’ own policies.
